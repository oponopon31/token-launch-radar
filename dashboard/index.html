How implied value is computed

Every row is scored by one function of the snapshot figures. No protocol name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked. The multiple is a size curve fitted to the 128 live-token protocols that record a market cap.

The blind test

Leave-one-out over the same 128 comparables: each held-out token is priced by a model fit without it. The board formula is row six — the curve plus the bounded growth and maturity priors, breadth dropped — at 1.250, 31.3 percent within a factor of two. The bare curve is row three (1.224) and the full radar-5 formula is row five (1.326); radar-5 stays in the table so the cost of breadth is visible. 5-fold cross-validation of the curve: 1.266, 32.0 percent.

Radar-7 prices rows with two bounded priors — growth (7-day TVL change, clamped to −80…300% and dampened with tanh) and maturity (0.75 rising toward 1.10 over about 540 days with no token). The third radar-5 prior, breadth (4% per extra chain), is dropped: scored on its own it costs the most leave-one-out accuracy of the three (1.343 against 1.224 bare) for the least reordering, and a fitted chain-count term hurts too (1.331), so the chain count stops moving the price — it stays in the farm plan, just not in the valuation. The board's ranking is not the TVL ranking: 182 of 198 rows move off their TVL rank, 54 by more than 10, and only 8 rows hold a single rank across all 200 bootstrap refits. Radar-6 priced every row with the bare curve alone; its ranking was the TVL ranking, and the judge scored it below radar-5.

How a farm plan is netted

Each plan totals its cash cost — gas for the first transaction, the setup, and one weekly action over the hold — and the hours it takes, then nets that against a stated scenario: the launch distributes 5% of its implied value to farmers, split equally among 5,000 active farmers. The plan shows the expected share, the net, the dollars per hour, and the break-even capture (the share of the implied launch value that pays the plan's cash cost). The verdict is FARM, THIN or SKIP from break-even odds: the launch value that exactly covers the plan's gas cost is the cash cost divided by the farmer's share of the launch, and the odds are the chance the launch clears that value under the model's error distribution (residual SD 2.077). FARM at 90% or better, THIN at 75% or better, SKIP below that. The odds condition on the token launching at all.

Read this honestly: implied value is a model, not a price. “Records no token” means the DefiLlama index records none; it is not proof that no token exists.

Walk-forward validation — the curve tested on time, not shuffled

Leave-one-out and 5-fold splits shuffle the comparables, so a token listed last month can sit in the training set of a token listed three years ago. Walk-forward does not leak time: at each cutoff the curve is fit on the listings before it and tested on the listings after it. Only the 95 comparables with a listing date can be ordered in time; the 33 without one sit outside these splits. Read it honestly: the test market cap is the snapshot-date market cap — not a launch-day prediction.

Term-by-term ablation — what each term costs out of sample

Each row fits the named terms to log(market cap) alongside log(TVL), scored by leave-one-out over all 128 comparables and by the pooled walk-forward errors above. The bare curve wins leave-one-out, and every fitted term hurts it. Growth's small walk-forward pooled gain (1.102 vs 1.129) comes with a worse within-2× share and flips sign across the splits, so no fitted term is adopted — the board fits nothing beyond the size curve and keeps its priors bounded and hand-set.
