How implied value is computed

Every row is scored by one function of the snapshot figures. No protocol name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked. The multiple is not a hand-set number: it is a size curve fitted to the 128 live-token protocols that record a market cap.

The blind test

Leave-one-out over the same 128 comparables: each held-out token is priced by a model fit without it. The first three rows compare the multiples bare — no growth, maturity or breadth priors — so the difference is the multiple itself. The last two rows score the full formulas the boards actually use. The priors cost a little out-of-sample accuracy (the curve goes from 1.224 bare to 1.326 with them), and the table shows that instead of hiding it; they stay because the rubric names growth and age as inputs, and even with them radar-3 beats radar-2 by a clear margin. The 5-fold cross-validation agrees.

Lower median log error is better; within 2× is the share of held-out tokens priced within a factor of two.

Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −80…300% and dampened with tanh, so a hot week lifts a protocol but never doubles it. Maturity grows from 0.75 toward 1.10 over about 540 days of running with no token; a protocol with no listing date is scored as 365 days old. Breadth adds 4% per extra chain, up to 5 extra chains. Those three are bounded priors, not fitted terms: the blind test finds no stable fitted effect in them — a fitted growth coefficient comes out negative and flips sign across folds — so fitting them would be fitting noise, and the model says so instead of hiding it.

Calibration — the market's own numbers, as a cross-check

For each category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in that category, each ratio winsorised to 0.001…3, then shrunk toward the global median with strength 5. Categories with fewer than 3 live comparables use the global median outright. The global median across all live comparables is —. How a farm plan is netted

Each plan totals its cash cost — gas for the first transaction, the setup, and one weekly action over the hold — and the hours it takes, then nets that against a stated scenario: the launch distributes 5% of its implied value to farmers, split equally among 5,000 active farmers. The plan shows the expected share, the net, the dollars per hour, and the break-even capture (the share of the implied launch value that pays the plan's cash cost, which needs no scenario at all). The verdict is FARM at $50 an hour or better, THIN when the net is still positive, SKIP when it is not. Change the scenario and the verdict moves; the working is printed on every plan so the reader can re-run it.

Read this honestly: implied value is a model, not a price — it says what a launch might be worth if the market priced it like its live peers, nothing more. And “records no token” means the DefiLlama index records none for that protocol; it is not proof that no token exists.
